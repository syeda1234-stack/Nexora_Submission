Transfer safety checklist

Before sending money with a licensed provider.

  • Compare the provider's offered rate and all fees.
  • Confirm recipient details through a trusted channel.
  • Check delivery time, refund policy and support contacts.
  • Keep the receipt and never share passwords or OTPs.
